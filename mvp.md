# mvp.md — Project V (3-day MVP)

**Working title:** *Do Explanations of Manipulation Policies Survive Dynamics Shift? Closed-Loop Faithfulness and Attribution-Shift Failure Prediction*

**Budget:** 3 days, ₹0 (Kaggle + Colab free tiers only). **Owner:** Bipin.

---

## 1. Goal

Ship a reproducible repo + short technical report showing, on simulated robot manipulation:

1. Which attribution methods are faithful in **closed loop**, not just open loop (C1), including the GWIE-C extension (C2).
2. Whether **shifts in attributions predict task failure** on held-out dynamics earlier/better than standard OOD and value signals (C3).
3. How **dynamics domain randomization changes what the policy relies on**, and whether DR+history policies encode dynamics parameters (C4).

---

## 2. Research questions and hypotheses

| RQ | Hypothesis | Test |
|---|---|---|
| RQ1 | Open-loop attribution rankings disagree with closed-loop importance, most for velocity groups. | Kendall τ(open, closed) per method < 0.6 for ≥3 of 6 methods; per-group disagreement table. |
| RQ2 | GWIE-C is at least as closed-loop-faithful as IG/SHAP and recovers the planted shortcut. | Closed-loop deletion pAUC; shortcut-detection rate. |
| RQ3 | Attribution shift predicts failure from the first 10/25 of 50 steps better than obs-OOD, action-std, Q, ensemble, activation-OOD. | AUROC with cluster-bootstrap 95% CI on test split. |
| RQ4 | DR (vs NoDR) spreads reliance across more groups; DR+History puts weight on past slots and encodes friction/mass linearly. | Attribution entropy, temporal share, probe R² vs raw-input and random-net controls. |

Outcomes are reported whichever way they fall.

### 4.2 Domain randomization (resampled each reset)

| Parameter | Implementation | NoDR | Train range | Held-out values (pre-calibration) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Object sliding friction | `model.geom_friction[obj,0]` × m | 1.0 | U[0.5, 1.5] | {0.2, 0.3, 2.0, 2.5} |
| Table friction | table geom × m | 1.0 | U[0.7, 1.3] | {0.4, 1.8} |
| Object mass | `model.body_mass[obj]` × m | 1.0 | U[0.5, 2.0] | {0.25, 3.0, 4.0} |
| Control gain | action × g (mocap displacement scale) | 1.0 | U[0.8, 1.2] | {0.5, 0.6, 1.5, 1.8} |
| Obs noise | N(0, σ²) on `observation` (m) | 0 | σ ∈ U[0, 0.005] | {0.01, 0.02} |
| Action latency | FIFO delay (steps) | 0 | {0, 1} | {2, 3} |

Held-out set = one-factor-at-a-time cells (others at nominal) + 20 random combined cells drawn outside train ranges (fixed seed).

**Day-1 calibration (then freeze):** with the pilot `push_dr` policy, adjust held-out *values only* so that DR-policy success in each cell lies in 20–80% (enough failures for C3, not trivially broken). Write `configs/dr/heldout.yaml`, record its hash. Never touch it again.

### 4.3 Evaluation

- Nominal + every held-out cell: 100 episodes per (policy, cell), deterministic policy (action mean).
- Per-step logs saved for all held-out episodes of `push_dr` and `push_drhist` (C3/C4) and for 200 nominal episodes per policy (calib pool).

## 5. C1 — Closed-loop faithfulness protocol

**Ground truth A — analytic controller (Reach and Push approach phase):**
`a_xyz = clip(Kp · (target − ee_pos) − Kd · ee_vel, −1, 1)`, gripper fixed. True relevant groups: `goal`/`target`, `ee_pos`, `ee_vel`; all object groups irrelevant. Analytic per-feature contribution = `Kp·(target−ee_pos)`, `−Kd·ee_vel`.
Metrics: precision@k of relevant groups, Spearman ρ vs analytic contributions, mass on irrelevant groups.

**Ground truth B — planted shortcut (Reach):** `shortcut_wrapper` appends `hint = goal + N(0, 0.002²)` to `observation` during training. At test, `hint` is (i) intact, (ii) replaced by a random point. Known reliance: success collapse under (ii) measures true dependence; methods are scored on the share they assign to `hint` vs `goal`.

**Learned Push policies (`push_dr`, seeds 0–2):**
- Open-loop importance per group: mean KL between π(·|s) and π(·|E_g(s)) over 2,000 states from the calib pool.
- Closed-loop importance per group: success drop when group g is erased (marginal resample, every step) for the whole episode, 100 episodes each; plus the redundant cluster `{ee_pos, obj_pos, obj_rel}` jointly.
- Deletion curves: erase top-k groups by each method's ranking (k=1..5) in closed loop → pAUC.
- Report: Kendall τ(method ranking, closed-loop ranking), τ(open-loop, closed-loop), deletion pAUC, all with bootstrap CIs.

Methods: `grad`, `grad_x_delta`, `ig`, `smoothgrad`, `occlusion`, `kernelshap_grouped`, `gwie_c` (+ `gwie_orig_port` if it differs).

## 7. C3 — Attribution-Shift Monitor (ASM)

**Signal per step t:** `z_t` = group-attribution share vector (IG, 16 steps, batched; 10 groups for Push).
**Nominal model:** mean/covariance of `z` over the calib split (nominal-dynamics episodes) for the same policy; Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage.
**Score:** `m_t = Mahalanobis(z_t)`; smoothed `e_t = EWMA_α(m_t)`, α chosen on calib; prefix score `S(T) = max_{t≤T} e_t`.
**Task:** predict episode failure from prefixes T ∈ {10, 25} (of 50 steps).

**Baselines (identical EWMA + prefix aggregation):**
1. `obs_ood` — Mahalanobis on raw observation.
2. `act_std` — mean of SAC actor `exp(log_std)`.
3. `q_drop` — negative of min-critic `Q(s, μ(s))`.
4. `ensemble` — std of action means across the 3 seed actors.
5. `act_ood` — Mahalanobis on actor penultimate activations.

**Metrics:** AUROC, AUPRC, TPR@5%FPR (threshold from calib), median detection step for failed episodes. Cluster bootstrap (seed → episode) 95% CIs. Also break down AUROC by held-out factor (friction / mass / gain / noise / latency).

**Policies evaluated:** `push_dr` (primary), `push_drhist`, `push_nodr` (secondary).

---

## 8. C4 — What DR changes

- **Reliance profile:** group shares (IG and GWIE-C) on nominal states for NoDR vs DR vs DR+Hist; attribution entropy; bootstrap CIs across seeds.
- **Temporal reliance (DR+Hist):** share on time slots t, t−1, t−2, t−3 and on past actions; nominal vs held-out cells.
- **Implicit system identification:** ridge probes (5-fold CV over episodes) predicting log friction, log mass, gain from (a) actor penultimate activations, (b) raw single-step obs (control), (c) raw stacked obs (control for DR+Hist), (d) random-init actor activations (control). Report R² with CIs. Claim implicit sysID only if (a) > (b) and (a) > (d) with non-overlapping CIs.
- **Robustness link:** held-out success per condition (mean ± bootstrap CI, per-seed shown), probability of improvement DR vs NoDR (`rliable`).
